§ 02

The wave

live · computed · 2D canvas

Three sine waves summed into a height field, then ordered-dithered up through the three spot inks — bare paper, then yellow, then cyan, with magenta only at the crests. It reads as a printing plate inking up, which is the language DESIGN.md already asked for, and it moves slowly enough to be atmosphere rather than an effect. The word below is genuinely knocked out of a paper sheet: the field runs behind the whole stage, and the sheet covers only the middle band, so you can see both at once.

Coverage Cell Speed
Where 01

The hero field

Full-bleed behind the first viewport, showing through the knockout word and in the open band above it. This is the one place it runs at full coverage.

Where 02

Section ramps

The diagonal speed stripes between bands become 30px slices of the same field — a hairline of movement that ties the page together without competing with anything.

Where 03

Project cards

A still frame, seeded per project, standing in for a screenshot. Recognisable as a plate, unreadable as a spec — which is how work projects get shown without being shown.

Never

Behind body copy

DESIGN.md's existing rule, and it holds. Every paragraph sits on opaque paper. A moving field under running text is unreadable and it's the one way this idea could ruin the page — so the field is always beside or above the prose, never under it.

Cost. One canvas, no dependencies, roughly 60 lines. It writes a typed array at cell resolution and scales it up with smoothing off, so a 1440-wide viewport at 5px cells is ~41k array writes and one drawImage per frame — about 1–2 ms. Capped at 30fps, paused when the tab is hidden or the hero scrolls out of view. Under prefers-reduced-motion it renders one composed frame and stops.
